How Your Digital Twin Works

5-Step Continuous Physiological Optimization Pipeline

The system builds a computational, calibrated version of you that models cardiovascular strain, biomechanical impact, and recovery dynamics in real time:

1
Collect

Stream live telemetry (heart rate, SpO2, 6-DOF IMU acceleration, cadence) from physical ESP32 wearables and longitudinal training logs.

2
Understand

Build your persistent athlete model, quantifying individual baseline resting HR, acute:chronic workload ratios (ACWR), and bilateral gait symmetry.

3
Predict

Use trained machine learning models to forecast future fatigue accumulation, recovery requirements, and readiness before you step onto the pitch.

4
Recommend

Generate personalized daily training focus, duration, and target intensity zones with transparent factor attribution.

5
Learn

Continuously calibrate the digital twin models as new session datasets arrive, refining individual tolerance and recovery kinetics over time.

Sensor Signal Processing & Bounds

• MAX30102 Optical PPG: Dual-wavelength (Red 660nm / IR 880nm) photoplethysmography with ambient background subtraction. Operating bounds: 30–240 BPM, 70–100% SpO2.
• MPU6050 6-DOF IMU: 3-axis accelerometer configured to +/- 2g scale (16,384 LSB/g) with dynamic gravity compensation.
• Packet Contract: Schema v2.4 strict runtime verification. Out-of-bounds readings are safely quarantined.
